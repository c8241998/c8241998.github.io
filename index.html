Zedong Chu / 储泽栋

Algorithm Expert & Team Lead
CV Lab, Alibaba AMAP, Beijing, China
I am an Algorithm Expert and Team Lead at Alibaba AMAP, focusing on Foundation Models and real-world Embodied AI applications. I received my M.Sc. from UCL under the supervision of Dr. Laura Toni. My first- or corresponding-author publications have appeared at top-tier venues including CVPR, ICLR, and IROS, highlighted by one CVPR Best Paper Candidate and several Oral/Highlight presentations. I also serve as a reviewer for journals and conferences such as IEEE TPAMI, CVPR, ECCV, NeurIPS, and IROS.
